BIO 2 Curriculum Module

Instructional Guide & Laboratory Protocol

Structured walkthrough for undergraduate General Biology II / Cell Biology learners and instructors.

Est. Lab Time: 25–35 Mins

Student Learning Objectives (SLOs)

  • SLO 1: Trace the co-translational import of secretory and lysosomal proteins via the SRP and Sec61 translocon complex.
  • SLO 2: Contrast vesicle coat machinery (COPII anterograde, COPI retrograde, and Clathrin post-Golgi).
  • SLO 3: Explain how Mannose-6-Phosphate (M6P) tags direct acid hydrolases from the Trans-Golgi Network (TGN) to lysosomes.
  • SLO 4: Describe membrane fusion specificity mediated by Rab-GTPases, tethering factors, and v-SNARE/t-SNARE pairing.

Guided 3D Simulation Walkthrough

STEP 1 — ER Translocation: Select Stage 1 and click the Rough ER preset. Observe bound ribosomes feeding nascent chains into the lumen where N-linked glycosylation occurs.
STEP 2 — COPII Budding: Advance to Stage 2. Watch Sar1-GTP recruit the purple polyhedral COPII cage at the ER Exit Site (ERES) and pinch off toward the cis-Golgi.
STEP 3 — Trans-Golgi Sorting: In Stage 3, toggle Coat Cages ON/OFF to inspect how cargo diverges at the TGN into pink Clathrin-coated lysosomal vesicles vs. green constitutive secretory vesicles.
STEP 4 — Targeted Fusion: In Stage 4, observe v-SNARE / t-SNARE engagement triggering exocytosis at the plasma membrane and hydrolase delivery to the acidic lysosome.

Core BIO 2 Molecular Glossary

Signal Recognition Particle (SRP)
Cytosolic ribonucleoprotein that binds the N-terminal ER signal peptide and halts translation until docking at the ER membrane.
KDEL Retrieval Sequence
C-terminal Lys-Asp-Glu-Leu tetrapeptide on resident ER lumenal proteins; captured by KDEL receptors in COPI vesicles if escaped to Golgi.
Mannose-6-Phosphate (M6P)
Oligosaccharide tag added in the cis-Golgi that marks soluble acid hydrolases for M6P receptor packaging into Clathrin vesicles.
SNARE Complex (v-SNARE & t-SNARE)
Transmembrane coiled-coil proteins that zipper together to overcome energy barriers and drive lipid bilayer fusion.
